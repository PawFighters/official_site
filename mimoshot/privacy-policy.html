MimoShot Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Paw Fighters LC. ("we", "us", or "our") operates the MimoShot app, a free, ad-supported service for searching procedure prices and clinics. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

You can search prices and clinics without an account. You need an account only to write reviews.

  • Account: When you sign in with Google, Apple, or Kakao, we receive an account identifier and, depending on the provider and your settings, your email address, name or nickname, and profile photo.
  • Profile: Your nationality and the nickname shown with your reviews.
  • Reviews: The rating and text of reviews you write.
  • Feedback: The title and message you send, and the app version.
  • Usage Data: Screens you view, searches, and taps; app version, operating system, device model, and language; IP address and the approximate location estimated from it; an anonymous identifier; and, if you are signed in, your member ID, nationality, and sign-in method.
  • Service Logs: IP address, device information, and access times recorded by our servers to operate and secure the service.
  • Advertising Data: Collected directly by Google AdMob. See Section 7.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To sign you in and manage your account.
  • To show your reviews to other users.
  • To respond to feedback and fix problems.
  • To analyze how the app is used and improve the service.
  • To show advertisements.

3. Public Information

Your nickname, profile photo, and reviews are visible to all users of the app. After you delete your account, your reviews stay visible with the author shown as "Deleted user", without your nickname or profile photo.

4. Retention and Deletion

  • Account and profile: Until your account is deleted.
  • Reviews and other content you post: Kept after your account is deleted, with the link to your account removed, so that other users can keep reading them.
  • Feedback: Up to 3 years from receipt.
  • Usage Data: 1 year from collection. The analytics profile linked to your account is kept until your account is deleted.
  • Service Logs: Until your account is deleted.

When the retention period ends or your account is deleted, we delete the information without delay so that it cannot be recovered, except for the content described above.

5. Service Providers

We entrust the following providers with processing on our behalf:

  • Supabase, Inc. (Republic of Korea): Cloud hosting and sign-in.
  • PostHog, Inc. (United States): Usage analytics.

These providers process your information only for the purposes above and under data protection terms that require protection equal to this policy.

6. Transfer Outside Korea

  • Recipient: PostHog, Inc. (privacy@posthog.com)
  • Country: United States
  • When and how: Sent over the network while you use the app
  • Items: The Usage Data listed in Section 1
  • Purpose: Analyzing and improving the service
  • Retention: 1 year from collection; the analytics profile linked to your account until your account is deleted
  • How to refuse: Email us at support@pawfighters.com to ask us to delete the analytics data linked to your account. This does not limit your use of the service.

7. Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google LLC collects device identifiers (such as the advertising ID, where available), IP address, and device information directly through the app to serve and measure ads, and keeps them under the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad personalization, in your device settings.

8. Security

We take administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, including access controls, limited access rights, and encryption in transit.

9. Children's Privacy

MimoShot is not directed to children under 14. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

10. Your Rights

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to stop its processing, including deleting your account and the data linked to it. Email us at support@pawfighters.com. We may ask for information to confirm that the request comes from you. See Delete Your Account for what is deleted and what is kept.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We will post any changes on this page with a new effective date.
