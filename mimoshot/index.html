01 / Price comparison

Compare clinics on one screen

See treatment prices from clinics around Gangnam Station and other supported areas, without visiting each clinic's website.

02 / Per-unit pricing

Compare prices on equal terms

Sort by per-unit prices, such as Rejuran Healer per 1cc, and check average and lowest rates to find a fair price benchmark.

Find clinics offering your treatment

Search for Botox, fillers, lasers, lifting, and more to find clinics that offer the treatment you're interested in.

04 / Promotional prices

See regular and promotional rates

View standard prices alongside promotional offers, so you can compare deals for the same treatment at a glance.

Prices and promotional terms may change. Confirm the final price and conditions with the clinic before treatment.
