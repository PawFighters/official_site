MimoShot Account Deletion

Delete Your Account

This page explains how to delete your account in MimoShot (미모샷), an app operated by Paw Fighters LC., and what happens to your data when you do.

1. How to Request Deletion

Email us at support@pawfighters.com with the subject "Delete my MimoShot account". Include:

  • The sign-in method you use in MimoShot (Google, Apple, or Kakao).
  • The email address of that account, or your MimoShot nickname.

We may ask for information to confirm that the request comes from you. We will email you when the deletion is complete.

2. Data We Delete

  • Account: Your account identifier, email address, name or nickname, and profile photo received from Google, Apple, or Kakao.
  • Profile: Your nationality and nickname.
  • Analytics profile: The usage analytics profile linked to your account.
  • Service Logs: Sign-in records linked to your account.

We delete this data without delay so that it cannot be recovered. We also disconnect MimoShot from your Kakao account. If you signed in with Apple or Google, you can also remove MimoShot from your Apple Account (Sign in with Apple settings) or your Google Account (third-party connections).

3. Data We Keep

  • Reviews and other content you post: Kept indefinitely with the link to your account removed. The author is shown as "Deleted user", without your nickname or profile photo.
  • Feedback: Messages sent through the app's feedback form are not linked to your account. They are kept for up to 3 years from receipt.
  • Usage Data: Usage events are kept for up to 1 year from collection, without the analytics profile linked to your account.
  • Server access logs: Deleted automatically after our hosting provider's log retention period (up to 90 days).

Advertising data is collected and kept by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

4. More Information

See the MimoShot Privacy Policy for details on how we handle your information.
