Botox for Teeth Grinding & TMJ

이갈이/턱관절

Relaxes jaw muscles overworked by grinding and clenching to ease pain and prevent damage.

Is it for you?

  • You grind or clench your teeth in your sleep
  • Your jaw feels stiff or sore in the morning
  • Clenching gives you frequent headaches
  • Tension in your jaw muscles is uncomfortable

What it is

Grinding and clenching are hard habits to break on purpose, and they strain your teeth and jaw joint. This treatment injects botulinum toxin into the chewing muscles to weaken them, reducing pain and wear on your teeth and jaw.

It works the same way as cosmetic jaw slimming, but the goal is to dial down excessive muscle force.

What it may help with

  • Less damage from grinding and clenching
  • Easing jaw tension and pain
  • Relief from related headaches
  • Bonus: your jawline may look slimmer

Why prices vary

The dose (units) depends on how developed your jaw muscles are, so prices vary. The product (Korean-made or imported) also matters, and treatment for medical reasons is usually not covered by Korean national health insurance.

When comparing, check that the product and number of units are the same.

What to expect

  1. 1Your grinding habits and jaw muscles are assessed.
  2. 2The injection area is disinfected.
  3. 3Botox is injected into the chewing muscles.
  4. 4After a quick briefing, you can go right back to your day.

Side effects & precautions

  • It doesn't stop the habit itself. The main effect is less muscle force, which means less damage.
  • Results usually start at 2–3 weeks and peak around month 3.
  • Bruising may occur. Avoid rubbing the area or taking hot baths afterward.
  • Chewing hard foods often can make the effect wear off faster.
  • An accurate diagnosis matters, so see a specialist if you have symptoms.

FAQ

Q Will I stop grinding completely?
The habit won't go away, but weaker muscles mean less damage to your teeth and jaw.
Q Is it the same as jaw-slimming Botox?
Same principle. The difference is the goal: cosmetic (smaller muscle) vs. functional (less force). Your jawline may look slimmer as a bonus.

This is general information about the treatment. Results and side effects vary from person to person. Always decide on treatment in consultation with a medical professional.
