Laser Genesis

레이저 제네시스

A treatment that warms the skin from within to boost collagen, refining pores, redness, and texture.

Is it for you?

  • Your pores are large and your texture is rough
  • Facial redness bothers you
  • You want care with no downtime
  • You want to address fine lines and firmness

What it is

Laser Genesis uses a 1064nm long-pulsed Nd:YAG laser. It's fired rapidly and repeatedly just above the skin to gently warm the dermis.

That heat triggers collagen production to refine pores, texture, and firmness. It doesn't remove skin or break up pigment, so it leaves almost no marks.

What it may help with

  • Shrinking enlarged pores
  • Better texture and firmness
  • Calming facial redness and flushing
  • Softer fine lines and acne scars

Why prices vary

Genesis is a technique, not a specific machine, so price depends on the device used (excel V, DermaV, Clarity, GentleMax, etc.). It also varies a lot depending on whether it's done alone or combined with toning or vascular modes. Since results build up, the package (number of sessions) matters too.

Side effects & precautions

  • You may notice mild redness or warmth afterward, which usually settles within a few hours.
  • Downtime is short, but rather than a big change at once, results come from several sessions.
  • Rarely, excess heat can cause burns or dark spots.
  • Be diligent about sun protection and moisturizing afterward.
  • Pores and redness are strongly tied to genetics and lifestyle, so they can come back.

FAQ

Q How is it different from laser toning?
Toning mainly fades pigment, while Genesis warms the dermis to refine pores, texture, and firmness. They're often done together.
Q Will I see results after one session?
Some people notice smoother texture after one, but pores and firmness usually improve clearly only after several sessions.

This is general information about the treatment. Results and side effects vary from person to person. Always decide on treatment in consultation with a medical professional.
