LDM Ultrasound

LDM 초음파

A gentle treatment that uses fine ultrasound vibrations to calm skin and support renewal.

Is it for you?

  • Your skin is sensitive and flushes easily
  • Your skin is dry with a weak barrier
  • You need soothing and recovery after a laser
  • Harsh treatments feel like too much

What it is

LDM rapidly alternates between ultrasound frequencies. These fine vibrations gently wake up activity in your skin cells.

It supports collagen and hyaluronic acid production to strengthen hydration and the skin barrier. It's very gentle, with almost no pain or downtime.

What it may help with

  • Calming sensitive, red skin
  • Better hydration and a stronger skin barrier
  • Firmer skin and better texture
  • Used to support recovery after laser treatments

At a glance

Treatment time
About 10–30 min (depending on the area)
Numbing
No numbing needed (very gentle)
Downtime
Almost none (back to your day with no redness or swelling)
How often
Usually 1–2 times a week at first, then once a month for maintenance (varies by person)
How long it lasts
It's about improving your skin's environment, so regular sessions work best

Why prices vary

LDM pricing depends on the area (full face or spot) and the number of sessions. It also varies between standalone care and combo programs with lasers or peels. Since it's ongoing care, packages are common.

What to expect

  1. 1Your skin's condition (sensitivity, dryness) is assessed.
  2. 2Gel or ampoule is applied and a handpiece is moved over the area.
  3. 3Ultrasound vibrations are delivered evenly.
  4. 4It finishes with soothing and moisturizing care.

Side effects & precautions

  • It's gentle and side effects are rare, but results show best with regular sessions rather than one.
  • It improves your skin's environment gradually rather than delivering an instant, dramatic change.
  • If you have wounds or an infection in the area, wait until it heals.
  • If combining with other treatments, discuss the order and timing with your doctor.

FAQ

Q Will I see results after one session?
Some people feel more hydrated and calm after one session, but firmness and barrier improvements show best with regular care.
Q Can I do it with laser treatments?
It's often paired with lasers to help soothe and repair sensitized skin. Ask your doctor about the order.

This is general information about the treatment. Results and side effects vary from person to person. Always decide on treatment in consultation with a medical professional.
