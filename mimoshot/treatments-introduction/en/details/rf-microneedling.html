Scars & firmness

RF Microneedling

RF 마이크로니들

A treatment that delivers RF heat into the dermis through fine needles to refine scars, pores, and firmness.

Is it for you?

  • You have acne scars or pitted marks
  • Your pores are large and your skin is sagging
  • Loss of firmness and fine lines bother you
  • You want scar treatment with less surface damage

What it is

RF microneedling inserts fine needles into the dermis. The needle tips release RF (radiofrequency) heat to stimulate the dermis.

It can deliver heat deep down while leaving the surface relatively untouched. That heat triggers collagen renewal to refine scars, pores, and firmness.

What it may help with

  • Improving acne scars and pitted marks
  • Shrinking enlarged pores
  • Firmer skin and softer fine lines
  • Depending on the tip, calming flushing and redness

At a glance

Treatment time
About 20–30 min (including time for numbing)
Numbing
Usually numbing cream
Downtime
A few days of redness, needle marks (bumps), and slight swelling; usually better within 2–3 days
How often
Usually 3–5 sessions, 3–4 weeks apart (varies by person; severe scars need more)
How long it lasts
New collagen lasts relatively long but gradually changes with aging

Why prices vary

RF microneedling pricing depends on the device (Potenza, Virtue, InMode, Sylfirm, etc.) and tip type. Even on the same device, tips for pores, scars, or firmness are priced differently. The area (spot vs. full face) and number of sessions (packages) also matter.

What to expect

  1. 1Your scars, pores, firmness, and skin thickness are assessed.
  2. 2Numbing cream is applied and left to work.
  3. 3Needle depth is set and RF is delivered area by area.
  4. 4Your skin is soothed, and you get instructions on repair cream and sun protection.

Side effects & precautions

  • Redness, needle marks, slight swelling, or tiny scabs may appear for a few days.
  • Scars and firmness don't improve in one go; most people notice change only after several sessions.
  • Rarely, dark spots, swelling, or temporary bruising can occur.
  • If you have dermatitis or infection in the area, a metal allergy, or an implanted electronic device (like a pacemaker), tell your doctor before treatment.
  • Be diligent about sun protection and moisturizing during recovery.

FAQ

Q How is it different from a laser?
Lasers stimulate the dermis with light; RF microneedling uses RF heat from the needle tips. It focuses on skin structure, like scars, pores, and firmness, more than pigment.
Q How many sessions do I need?
Results usually become clear after several sessions, and the number depends on how deep your scars are.

This is general information about the treatment. Results and side effects vary from person to person. Always decide on treatment in consultation with a medical professional.
