Calf Botox

종아리

Relaxes bulky calf muscles for a smoother, slimmer leg line.

Is it for you?

  • Your calf muscles are firm and prominent
  • You want a smoother leg line
  • Your calves bulge when you stand on tiptoe
  • You want to reshape your legs without surgery

What it is

Prominent calves are often caused by well-developed muscle. Calf Botox injects botulinum toxin into these muscles to reduce movement so they gradually shrink.

Note: it does little for calves that are mostly fat rather than muscle.

What it may help with

  • Less bulky calf muscles
  • A neater leg line
  • Less prominent calves
  • A smoother silhouette

At a glance

Usually about 5–6 months (varies by person)

Why prices vary

Calf Botox uses a relatively large dose, so the number of units makes a big difference in price. It also depends on whether the product is Korean-made or imported, or one designed to lower the risk of resistance.

When comparing, check that the product and number of units are the same.

What to expect

  1. 1Your calves are checked to confirm they're muscle-type.
  2. 2The injection area is disinfected.
  3. 3It's injected at several points in the calf muscle.
  4. 4After a quick briefing, you can go right back to your day.

Side effects & precautions

  • Results appear gradually from about a month and become clear at 2–4 months.
  • It barely works on fat-type calves, so checking your type first is important.
  • You may have bruising or temporary stiffness.
  • Avoid strenuous exercise for a few days afterward.
  • Tell your doctor before treatment if you have a neuromuscular condition or are pregnant or breastfeeding.

FAQ

Q Will I see results quickly?
It works by shrinking muscle, so you'll need about a month to notice a change.
Q Will it affect my walking?
At an appropriate dose, it usually doesn't interfere much with daily life. Your doctor can assess your case.

This is general information about the treatment. Results and side effects vary from person to person. Always decide on treatment in consultation with a medical professional.
