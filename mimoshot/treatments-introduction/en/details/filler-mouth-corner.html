Mouth Corner Filler

입꼬리

Supports drooping mouth corners from below for a softer, brighter expression.

Is it for you?

  • The corners of your mouth turn down
  • You look sad when your face is relaxed
  • You want a softer, friendlier look
  • You want a neater line around your mouth

What it is

Drooping mouth corners can make you look sad or older even at rest. Mouth corner filler adds hyaluronic acid around the corners to lift and support the line.

It's similar to lip filler, but focuses on the mouth corners rather than the lips.

What it may help with

  • Lifting drooping mouth corners
  • A less sad-looking expression
  • More volume around the mouth
  • A softer, brighter expression

At a glance

Treatment time
About 10–20 min
Numbing
Numbing cream (if needed)
Downtime
Bruising and swelling usually within a few days
Recommended cycle
Top up when it starts to fade
How long it lasts
Usually 6 months to 1 year (varies by product)

Why prices vary

Pricing depends on the amount (cc) and filler type (Korean-made or imported). It also varies depending on whether only the mouth corners are treated or the lips too.

When comparing, first check that the treatment area and amount are the same.

What to expect

  1. 1How much your mouth corners droop is assessed in consultation.
  2. 2Numbing cream is applied and the area is disinfected.
  3. 3Small amounts of filler are injected around the mouth corners.
  4. 4Both sides are fine-tuned for balance.

Side effects & precautions

  • Bruising, swelling, or pain may occur at the injection sites.
  • The mouth area moves a lot, so results may not last as long as in other areas.
  • Too much filler can look unnatural or uneven.
  • If there's a problem, hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved with an enzyme.

FAQ

Q How is it different from mouth corner Botox?
Filler fills in hollow areas, while Botox relaxes the muscle that pulls the corners down. They're sometimes combined, depending on your face.
Q Does it wear off quickly?
The mouth area moves a lot, so it may not last as long. Ask about expected duration in your consultation.

This is general information about the treatment. Results and side effects vary from person to person. Always decide on treatment in consultation with a medical professional.
