Treatment Guide
Skin booster

Rejuran HB

리쥬란HB

A skin booster that adds numbing and hyaluronic acid to Rejuran Healer for less pain and more glow.

Is it for you?

  • You want Rejuran but worry about pain
  • You want hydration and glow on top of skin renewal
  • Loss of firmness and fine lines bother you
  • Your skin is thin and dry

What it is

Rejuran HB (HB plus) adds two things to PN (polynucleotides), the main ingredient in Rejuran Healer. It contains lidocaine, a local anesthetic, to reduce pain during treatment, and hyaluronic acid (HA) for added hydration and glow. The core skin-renewal principle is the same as Rejuran Healer.

What it may help with

  • Firmer skin
  • Softer fine lines and smoother texture
  • Better hydration and glass-skin glow
  • Relatively less pain during treatment

At a glance

Treatment time
About 20–30 min
Numbing
Numbing cream plus lidocaine in the formula
Downtime
Injection marks and redness for about 2–3 days; bruising or swelling about a week for some
How often
Usually every 2–4 weeks, 3–4 sessions
How long it lasts
Varies by person, but about 6–12 months

Why prices vary

Like other Rejuran products, pricing depends on the amount, treatment area, single session vs. package, and whether it's injected by hand or with an injector device. With added lidocaine and hyaluronic acid, it may be priced differently from regular Rejuran Healer.

When comparing, check that it's the same product, amount, and number of sessions.

What to expect

  1. 1After cleansing, numbing cream is applied and left on for a while.
  2. 2The area is disinfected.
  3. 3A formula containing PN, HA, and lidocaine is injected into the dermis.
  4. 4Your skin is soothed to finish.

Side effects & precautions

  • You may see redness, swelling, or bruising where it was injected.
  • If you've had allergies to lidocaine or other ingredients, you must tell your clinic beforehand.
  • Avoid alcohol, saunas, and intense exercise for a few days afterward.
  • If you're pregnant or breastfeeding, or have an infection or inflammation in the area, talk to your doctor before treatment.

FAQ

Q How is it different from Rejuran Healer?
It's Rejuran Healer with added lidocaine (numbing) and hyaluronic acid (hydration). Less pain, more glow.
