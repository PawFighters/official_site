Redness & vessels

Vascular & Redness Laser

혈관·홍조 레이저

A laser that targets and calms the blood vessels behind redness, flushing, and broken capillaries.

Is it for you?

  • Your face flushes easily (facial redness)
  • You have visible broken capillaries on your nose or cheeks
  • Your acne has left red marks
  • Red scars or flushing bother you

What it is

Vascular lasers target hemoglobin in blood vessels, not pigment. Light of a specific wavelength is absorbed only by the vessels, and the heat coagulates them so they fade.

Devices and wavelengths such as Vbeam (595nm), excel V, DermaV, and Clarity reach different vessel depths. Modes are switched to treat everything from surface capillaries to deeper vessels.

What it may help with

  • Calming facial flushing and redness
  • Improving broken capillaries on the nose and cheeks (telangiectasia)
  • Fading red acne marks
  • Lightening red scars

At a glance

Treatment time
About 10–20 min (depending on the area)
Numbing
Built-in cooling, so usually no numbing
Downtime
Usually minimal (temporary redness); some settings cause a few days of bruising (purpura)
How often
Usually every 2–4 weeks, 3–5 or more sessions (varies by person)
How long it lasts
Redness and vessels can come back depending on genetics and lifestyle

Why prices vary

Vascular laser pricing depends on the device (Vbeam, excel V, DermaV, Clarity, etc.) and wavelength. Even for redness, full face vs. spots (nose, cheeks) and the mode (vascular, pigment, or Genesis) make a difference. Redness often takes several sessions, so single sessions vs. packages matter too.

What to expect

  1. 1The vessels, redness, and your skin are assessed.
  2. 2You put on eye protection.
  3. 3The laser is applied to the vessels while the skin is cooled.
  4. 4Your skin is soothed and sunscreen is applied.

Side effects & precautions

  • Temporary redness or swelling may appear and usually settles within hours to a few days.
  • Depending on the setting and device, bruising (purpura) may last a few days and fade over time.
  • Rarely, dark spots, blisters, or scabs can form.
  • Redness and vessels won't vanish in one go and can come back depending on your genetics.
  • Before and after treatment, avoid things that dilate blood vessels, like saunas, alcohol, and strong sun.

FAQ

Q Will my redness go away completely?
It helps reduce redness, but flushing is strongly tied to genetics and lifestyle, so ongoing care is often needed.
Q Will I definitely bruise?
Most clinics now use settings that minimize bruising. For stubborn, dark vessels, though, a bruising setting is sometimes used on purpose for better results.

This is general information about the treatment. Results and side effects vary from person to person. Always decide on treatment in consultation with a medical professional.
