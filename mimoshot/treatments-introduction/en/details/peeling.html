Chemical Peel

필링

A treatment that uses acids to dissolve dead skin and refine texture and tone.

Is it for you?

  • Flaky skin feels rough
  • Your skin tone looks dull
  • Pores, oil, and breakouts bother you
  • You want gentle exfoliation

What it is

A peel uses acids (AHA, BHA, PHA, LHA) to gently dissolve dead skin. It loosens the bonds holding dead skin cells together so they shed naturally.

Clogged pores clear up and texture and tone become smoother. Different acids vary in strength and suit different skin types.

What it may help with

  • Smoother texture by clearing dead skin
  • Brightening dull skin
  • Clearing pores, oil, and blackheads
  • Fading dark spots and calming breakouts

Why prices vary

Peel pricing depends on the acid (AHA, BHA, PHA, LHA, etc.) and strength. Even for the same peel, area (full face or spot) and add-ons (soothing care, ampoules, etc.) make a difference. Since it's ongoing care, packages are common.

Side effects & precautions

  • Mild flaking, redness, or tightness may follow and usually settles within a few days.
  • Stronger acids work harder but also cause more irritation and peeling.
  • Your skin is more sensitive to sunlight after a peel, so sun protection is especially important.
  • Avoid scrubs, strong exfoliants, and harsh skincare for a few days afterward.
  • Treatment may be limited on areas with dermatitis, wounds, or severe breakouts.

FAQ

Q Won't frequent peels make my skin thinner?
Following the right schedule for the acid and strength is usually fine. Some gentle peels are even designed to strengthen the barrier. What matters is matching it to your skin.
Q What's the difference between AHA, BHA, and PHA?
Broadly, AHA works on surface dead skin and tone, BHA works on oil inside pores, and PHA and LHA are gentler. The right one depends on your skin type.

This is general information about the treatment. Results and side effects vary from person to person. Always decide on treatment in consultation with a medical professional.
