A HIFU lift that delivers dot and line ultrasound heat to the SMAS to lift sagging skin.

Is it for you?

  • Your jawline and cheeks are sagging
  • You want lifting tailored to each area
  • You want lifting without surgery
  • You want to firm your brows and body too

What it is

ULTRAcel Q+ is a HIFU device made by Korean company Jeisys Medical. It delivers focused ultrasound in dot and line patterns to the SMAS about 3–4.5mm below the skin, creating heat points (about 70°C or higher). This makes tissue contract and renews collagen for firmer skin.

What it may help with

  • A tighter jawline and cheek contour
  • Firmer skin
  • Softer fine lines

Side effects & precautions

  • You may feel stinging or heat during treatment, and temporary redness or swelling afterward.
  • Rarely, bruising or temporary changes in sensation can occur.
  • Treatment may not be possible if you have an implanted electrical medical device.
  • Results and recovery time vary from person to person.

FAQ

Q How is it different from LinearZ?
Both are Jeisys HIFU devices. They differ in delivery method and depth, so the choice depends on the area you want to treat.
