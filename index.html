About

We're a small team of senior full-stack engineers. We use strong engineering and AI to solve everyday problems with products that feel simple.

We take products from idea to App Store ourselves: research, design, app, backend, data, and launch. Staying small is a choice. It keeps us fast, focused, and close to every detail. We believe not everything needs to be big to be meaningful.

What we stand for

  1. 01
    Keep it small We stay small on purpose. Senior engineers own the whole stack, with no hand-offs and no layers, so every decision stays quick and every detail gets attention.
  2. 02
    Engineering-first problem solving We look for the problem that technology can actually fix. In Mimoshot, that meant turning messy clinic packages into one comparable unit price.
  3. 03
    AI in every workflow We build with AI from research to code to content, so a few people can ship what used to take a much larger team.
  4. 04
    Ship, measure, iterate We release early, watch how people really use our apps, and improve in short cycles.
  5. 05
    Honest by default Clear information, no dark patterns, and only the data a product truly needs.
