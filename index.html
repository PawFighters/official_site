About

Paw Fighters is a small team with a sharp focus: creating simple, essential apps that solve real problems.

We believe that not everything needs to be big to be meaningful. In a world filled with distractions and complexity, we find purpose in building tools that feel quiet, honest, and human. We start from frustrations we have felt ourselves, and we keep each product small enough to do one job well.

Today our main focus is Mimoshot, an app that makes aesthetic clinic prices in Korea easier to compare by converting every offer into a fair, per-unit price.
